FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which file types work?

JPG, PNG, WEBP and PDF. A scanned plan or a clear phone photo of a printed plan works too. Plans with printed room names and sizes give the best result.

Does it handle multi-storey plans?

Yes. Upload one image per floor, or one sheet with all floors on it. DIMORA stacks the floors and connects them with the staircase.

Is my plan private?

Your plan is used only to build your home. Your saved homes are visible only to you (each account can read only its own data). Share links are view-only and are created only when you click "Share". You can delete your account and all your data from Account settings.

Which AI does it use?

Powered by Google Gemini. Gemini reads your plan, powers the Aria assistant and the "3 layout ideas", and a Gemini image model makes the "Make it Real" photos (they carry Google's SynthID watermark).

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser on a laptop or phone. You can also install it like an app from the browser menu.

Are the Vastu hints and costs exact?

No — they are general guidance and estimates to help you decide, not professional, structural or religious advice.
